# Code of Conduct

heydb follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any project space.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/pvidaal07/heydb/security/advisories/new) or by contacting [@pvidaal07](https://github.com/pvidaal07). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments or block contributors who do not follow this code.
